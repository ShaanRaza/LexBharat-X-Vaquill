=== SECTION TEXT (paste below this line) ===
The principal must make compensation to his agent in respect of injury caused to such agent by the principal’s neglect or want of skill.

Illustration
A employs B as a bricklayer in building a house, and puts up the scaffolding himself. The scaffolding is unskilfully put up, and B is in consequence hurt. A must make compensation to B.
