–The [State Government] *** may make rules for regulating––
(a) the supply and sale of stamps an stamped papers, (b) the persons by whom alone such sale is to be conducted, and
(c) the duties and remuneration of such persons:
Provided that such rules shall not restrict the sale of [ten nayepaise or five nayapaise] adhesive stamps.
