Description of instrument: APPRAISEMENT OR VALUATION made otherwise than under an order of the Court in the course of a suit— (a) where the amount does not exceed Rs. 1,000; (b) in an other case……………………………. Exemptions (a) Appraisement or valuation made for the information of one party only, and not being in any manner obligatory between parties either by agreement or operation of law. (b) Appraisement of crops for the purpose of ascertaining the amount to be given to a landlord as rent.
Proper stamp-duty: The same duty as a Bond (No. 15) for such amount. Five rupees.
