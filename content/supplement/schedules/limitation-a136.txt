Description of suit: For the execution of any decree (other than a decree granting a mandatory injunction) or order of any civil court.
Period of limitation: Twelve years.
Time from which period begins to run: [When] the decree or order becomes enforceable or where the decree or any subsequent order directs any payment of money or the delivery of any property to be made at a certain date or at recurring periods, when default in making the payment or delivery in respect of which execution is sought, takes place: Provided that an application for the enforcement or execution of a decree granting a perpetual injunction shall not be subject to any period of limitation.
