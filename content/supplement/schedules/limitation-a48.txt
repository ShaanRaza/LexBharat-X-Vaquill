Description of suit: For contribution by a party who has paid the whole or more than his share of the amount due under a joint decree, or by a sharer in a joint estate who has paid the whole or more than his share of the amount of revenue due from himself and his co-sharers.
Period of limitation: Three years.
Time from which period begins to run: The date of the payment in excess of the plaintiff’s own share.
