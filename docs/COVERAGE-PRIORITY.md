# Coverage priority: Central Acts by Supreme Court citations

Generated by `scripts/acts/coverage-priority.mjs` from `scripts/acts/sc-citations-scan.mjs` on 2026-10-29. **Reports only; nothing was fetched or changed.**

**How the ranking works.** The scan read the full text of all 35,596 Supreme Court judgments in our database. An Act's number is the count of DISTINCT judgments that name it ("Indian Penal Code, 1860" and "Penal Code, 1860" are added together; short forms such as IPC, CrPC, CPC and NI Act count for the 17 Acts listed in `src/lib/judgments/statuteLinks.mjs`). The names are matched to our Central Acts by title and year, as in `rank-central-acts.mjs`. The "top sections" are the sections cited most often together with that Act (the number in brackets is the judgments citing it); they are found by regex on prose, so treat them as a ranking, not an exact count. Judgments decided before 2024 cannot cite the new codes (BNS, BNSS, BSA), so those rank lower than their real importance; they stay on the download list for that reason.

**Status.** *complete*: no gap found. *missing N sections*: numbers absent from 1..N by the rule in `docs/MISSING-SECTIONS.md` (most are "uncertain"; "confident" ones are the ones the Act page warns about). *garbled*: sections whose text is scrambled in the source. *absent*: the Act has no text in our library. **Needs a download** = absent, or a confident gap, or garbled text, or a section cited in 10 or more judgments that we do not hold (or hold scrambled). The top-20 order is the sum, over the sections we lack, of the judgments citing each (a judgment citing two such sections counts twice); an absent Act counts every section cited.
