LEXBHARAT HARD EVALUATION QUESTIONS - FOR LAWYER REVIEW (draft, written by Claude, not yet checked)
For each: the question as a user would type it, the section(s) the answer should rest on, and a note. Please mark each OK / change / remove.

101. [plain] My tenant isn't paying rent, what can I do?
   Expected: The Transfer of Property Act - section 106 or 111
   The answer must mention: rent[- ]control
   Note: Accept TPA s.106 or s.111 (s.106 is garbled in our data, so only s.111 can be matched). A good answer must say that eviction for non-payment of rent is mainly governed by State rent-control law (checked by the words 'rent control'). Lawyer: confirm.

102. [plain] A car hit my father and drove away. How do we claim compensation?
   Expected: The Motor Vehicles Act - section 161
   Note: s.161 (hit and run compensation scheme); s.166 (application for compensation) also relevant.

103. [plain] Can the police arrest me without a warrant?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - section 35
   Note: BNSS s.35 (CrPC s.41). Also s.35(3) notice of appearance for offences up to 7 years.

104. [plain] I lent my friend money three years ago. Is it too late to file a case to get it back?
   Expected: The Limitation Act - section Schedule, Art. 19 (also accepted: Limitation Act s.18; Limitation Act s.19)
   Note: Art. 19 of the Schedule: three years from when the loan is made (Art. 21 if payable on demand). Section 3 and 5 also relevant. Data note: Limitation Act has only 43 sections in the data. Also accepted: Limitation Act s.18 (acknowledgment) and s.19 (part payment).

105. [plain] I think I may be arrested in a false case. Can I get bail before they arrest me?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - section 482
   Note: Anticipatory bail, BNSS s.482 (CrPC s.438).

106. [plain] My husband beats me. What law protects me?
   Expected: The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act - section 18
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 85 (also accepted: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita s.115)
   Note: PWDVA s.18 protection orders (also s.3 definition, s.12 application) and BNS s.85 (cruelty by husband or relatives; IPC 498A). Also accepted for the criminal part: BNS s.115 (voluntarily causing hurt).

107. [plain] What is the punishment for drunk driving?
   Expected: The Motor Vehicles Act - section 185
   Note: MV Act s.185.

108. [plain] Can I file for divorce in the first year of marriage?
   Expected: The Hindu Marriage Act - section 14
   Note: HMA s.14 (one-year bar, with leave for exceptional hardship). Applies to Hindus; other communities have their own Acts.

109. [hinglish] cheque bounce ho gaya kya karu
   Expected: The Negotiable Instruments Act - section 138
   Note: NI Act s.138: written demand notice within 30 days of the bank's return memo, 15 days to pay, then complaint within one month (s.142).

110. [hinglish] police meri FIR darj nahi kar rahi, ab kya karu
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - section 173
   Note: BNSS s.173 (CrPC s.154): send to the Superintendent of Police (s.173(4)), then Magistrate (s.175(3)).

111. [hinglish] kisi ne mera phone chura liya, uski saza kya hai
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 303
   Note: BNS s.303(2) theft. Snatching is s.304 - check which the lawyer prefers for a stolen phone.

112. [hinglish] dahej ke liye sasural wale pareshan kar rahe hain, kaunsi dhara lagegi
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 85
   Expected: The Dowry Prohibition Act - section 4 (expected; 3 also accepted)
   Note: Dowry Prohibition Act s.4 (demanding dowry) is the expected section and s.3 is also accepted. DATA: our s.4 is garbled (only footnote text), so the scorer can match only s.3 until the PDF is loaded (docs/PDF-DOWNLOAD-LIST.md row 40). BNS s.85-86 (cruelty incl. dowry demand); BNS s.80 only if death.

113. [hinglish] non bailable case mein bail kaise milti hai
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - section 480 (also accepted: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita s.483)
   Note: BNSS s.480 (CrPC s.437). Also accepted: BNSS s.483 (CrPC s.439).

114. [hinglish] RTI ka jawab nahi aaya to appeal kahan karni hai
   Expected: The Right to Information Act - section 19
   Note: RTI Act s.19(1): first appeal to the officer senior to the PIO within 30 days; second appeal s.19(3) to the Information Commission within 90 days.

115. [two-act] Someone cheated people online using my password. What is the punishment, and how do I prove the messages in court?
   Expected: The Information Technology Act - section 66C (expected; 66D accepted) (also accepted: Information Technology Act s.66D)
   Expected: The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam - section 63
   Note: IT Act s.66C (identity theft: using someone's password) expected, s.66D (cheating by personation) accepted, plus BSA s.63 for proving electronic records. IT Act s.66 and s.43 are missing in the data.

116. [two-act] A company's director signed a cheque that bounced. Is the director personally liable, and can it also be cheating?
   Expected: The Negotiable Instruments Act - section 141
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 318
   Note: NI Act s.141 (vicarious liability, in charge and responsible) and BNS s.318 (cheating, IPC 420) - cheating needs dishonest intention at the start.

117. [two-act] Is a contract signed by a 16 year old valid, and can a child be punished for a crime?
   Expected: The Indian Contract Act - section 11
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 20
   Note: Contract Act s.11 (minor's agreement void), BNS s.20 (under 7) and s.21 (7 to 12 with immature understanding); Juvenile Justice Act 2015 also matters for 16-year-olds, not in this set.

118. [two-act] My husband is cruel and does not support me. Can he be punished and can I get monthly maintenance?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 85
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita - section 144
   Note: BNS s.85 (IPC 498A) and BNSS s.144 (CrPC s.125).

119. [two-act] Does the limitation period apply to arbitration, and how long do I have to file a suit for a loan I gave?
   Expected: The Arbitration and Conciliation Act - section 43
   Expected: The Limitation Act - section Schedule, Art. 19
   Note: Arbitration s.43(1) and Limitation Act Schedule Art. 19 (3 years). Arbitration s.34 is mangled in the data (Schedule text), so not used.

120. [bns-vs-ipc] What is the punishment for murder under IPC section 302?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 103
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.302
   Note: IPC 302 is repealed from 1 July 2024; the current law is BNS s.103(1). Answer should say 'previously IPC s.302'.

121. [bns-vs-ipc] IPC 420 cheating punishment
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 318
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.420
   Note: BNS s.318(4) (cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property): up to 7 years and fine. Old IPC 420.

122. [bns-vs-ipc] Is section 498A of the IPC still law?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 85
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.498A
   Note: Replaced by BNS s.85 and s.86 (cruelty defined).

123. [bns-vs-ipc] What is the new section for criminal conspiracy, earlier 120B?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 61
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.120B
   Note: BNS s.61 (IPC 120A and 120B).

124. [bns-vs-ipc] What is the punishment for theft under IPC section 379?
   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 303
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.379
   Note: BNS s.303(2): up to 3 years, or fine, or both; community service for first-time theft under Rs 5,000 if the property is returned. Old IPC 379: up to 3 years.

131. [constitutional] Police are illegally detaining my brother, which court do I go to?
   Expected: The Constitution of India - section Art. 226 (Art. 32 accepted) (also accepted: Constitution of India s.Art. 32)
   Note: Habeas corpus: High Court under Art. 226 (usual first step) or Supreme Court under Art. 32. BNSS s.35-36 and Art. 22 also relevant. Lawyer: confirm which is expected first.

   Expected: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - section 356
   Old-law rule: answer from the current law and say it was previously IPC s.499
   Note: BNS s.356 (defamation; previously IPC s.499/500). The IT Act's old s.66A is struck down and not relevant. The answer should name the old section as 'previously IPC s.499'.

   Note: POSH Act 2013 s.9 (complaint to the Internal Committee; s.4 constitution of the Internal Committee, s.6 Local Committee). BNS s.75 also relevant. Lawyer: confirm.
