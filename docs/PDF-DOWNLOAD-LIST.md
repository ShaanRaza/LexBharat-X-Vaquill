# India Code PDFs to download (group B: Act text we cannot fill from the dataset)

**What this is.** The Acts below are stubs, missing, short of sections, or have Schedules / Forms / Appendices we do not hold. Each needs the Act's own PDF from India Code (https://www.indiacode.nic.in), saved into `content/supplement/pdfs/` under the exact file name given. Then the same extractor used for the Contract Act, CPC, Limitation, Stamp and the Constitution (`scripts/supplement/`) fills the missing sections and Schedule units, word-by-word checked against the PDF.

**Order (updated from real use).** The rows are ranked by how many Supreme Court judgments cite a section of that Act which we lack (or, for an Act not in our library, how many name it): see `docs/COVERAGE-PRIORITY.md` for the method. Pri 1 = 200 or more, Pri 2 = 40 or more, Pri 3 = the rest. The `#` numbers did not change. Judgments before 2024 cannot cite the new codes (BNS, BNSS, BSA), so those rows rank low on this measure though they matter for current law; download them anyway.

**Rules.** Download by hand, in your browser (the site refuses automated downloads, and we do not work around that). Take the **"as on" / latest** PDF of the Act, not a bare/original-as-enacted version, unless the row says otherwise. Do not edit the PDF. One PDF per Act. Priority 1 = most used by practising lawyers. Max 40 Acts (this list has 40 rows).

**How to find each Act on India Code.** Search by the exact title below (the title as India Code prints it), or use the Act-year filter / enactment-date filter with the range in the "Date filter" column (enactment date from – to; the Act number is shown in brackets for checking you opened the right one). Open the Act, then the PDF link (the "Act as a whole" PDF, not a single section).

## Notes
- **Act numbers and years in brackets are from memory** and are only a way to check you opened the right Act; where the row says "check", I am not certain of the number or the exact title, and the 2025 titles may be printed differently on India Code. If India Code does not have an Act, skip the row and tell me.
- **Already on disk, no download needed:** Contract Act 1872, CPC 1908 (the Appendices), Hindu Marriage Act 1955, Limitation Act 1963, NI Act 1881, Prevention of Corruption Act 1988, Specific Relief Act 1963, Stamp Act 1899, Constitution of India (see `content/supplement/pdfs/SOURCES.md`).
- **Not on this list on purpose:** Rules made under the Acts (Supreme Court Rules, Companies Rules, IT Rules and so on), State Acts, and amending Acts. They are separate documents on India Code; say if you want a second list.
- **After you save a PDF:** tell me the file name. I run the extractor for that Act (same checks as before: every word must be in the PDF, section by section), load the new units, and re-run the missing-number checker (`node scripts/w10/missing-all-central.mjs`).
- **Permission.** Whether we may republish India Code text is still open (see `docs/INDIA-CODE-PERMISSION-EMAIL.md`). Downloading a PDF for your own checking is fine; loading its text onto the site waits on that answer, as for the first 8 Acts.
