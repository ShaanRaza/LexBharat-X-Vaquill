## 1. Constitution of India: blocked
`content/supplement/pdfs/constitution-of-india.pdf` (saved as `constitution-of-india.pdf.pdf`, renamed) is **the hand-lettered, illuminated original of 1950**: 234 pages that are pictures of calligraphy, no text layer, and none of the amendments (no Article 21A, no Part IXA/IXB, no 243ZH). Extracting it word by word is impossible, and the text would be out of date. Its India Code address is recorded in `content/supplement/pdfs/SOURCES.md` with this warning. **What is needed:** India Code's normal text PDF of "The Constitution of India" (amended text, "as on <date>"), saved as `content/supplement/pdfs/constitution-of-india.pdf`. I did not download anything myself (India Code blocks automated downloads, and downloads need your yes).

What is already in place for when it arrives: the supplement loader accepts Article units `A21`, `A21A`, `A243ZH` and Schedule units `S7`; lookups understand "Article 21", "Art. 226 Constitution", "Article 32", "Art 14", "Article 21A", "Seventh Schedule" (they fall back to ordinary search while the Act is missing); chat spots "Article N" in questions; statute links in judgments already recognise Constitution mentions (1,680 judgments) and only need the Act ID in `src/config/statute-acts.json` (`constitution`). Still to write when the PDF is here: the extractor for its layout (Parts, Chapters, Articles, Schedules) and the word-by-word check, and Parts/Chapters as headings on the Act page.
