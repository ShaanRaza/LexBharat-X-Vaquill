## 1. Constitution of India
The first file (79 MB, saved as `constitution-of-india.pdf.pdf`) was the picture-only 1950 manuscript and was refused. The second file is India Code's text PDF (title "Constitution of India_ 20 MAY 2026 (final).pdf", 402 pages, "As on 10th September, 2026", the Legislative Department pocket edition). It includes **all amendments up to the 106th (2023)**, the latest Constitution Amendment Act we know of, so it is not older than the latest amendments (check India Code if a 107th is ever passed). Source URL and this note are in `content/supplement/pdfs/SOURCES.md` and in every file's header.

**What was added** (`scripts/supplement/constitution.mjs`, `fill-constitution.mjs`; same line reader and word-by-word check as the Acts, plus a completeness check):
- **500 Articles**, including the lettered ones (21A, 31A–31D, 243ZH, 371J, 394A ...), the omitted ones (shown as printed: "Omitted by the Constitution (Forty-fourth Amendment) Act ...") and 108 with letters; and **12 Schedules** (First to Twelfth) as units. Each is a unit `A21`, `A243ZH`, `S7` in `content/supplement/schedules/constitution-*.txt` (512 files).
- Parts, Chapters and cross-headings ("PART III — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS · Right to Freedom") are stored with each unit and shown as headings in the Act page's list and under each title.
- The Act itself is a new row in `acts` (`IND_central_COI`, added by the loader, never touching existing rows) and an entry in the Acts list data (`src/data/acts/acts.legislation.json`).
- Checks: all 512 units pass the word-by-word check against the PDF's own text (0 failed); the completeness check (words printed for the Article that are missing from the unit, page headings, footnotes and the Article number excluded) found **0 Articles with more than 3% of their words missing**. Page headings ("9 THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA", "(Part III.—Fundamental Rights)") and footnotes are removed; one footnote printed inside a line of Article 334 is cut out by pattern.
- Not included: the Preamble, and Appendices I to III (the 100th Amendment's land-boundary Schedules, the 2019 Order and the Article 370(3) declaration). The Schedules are kept as printed lines, not as grids (the First, Fourth and Seventh Schedules are tables in the book).
- A long heading wrapped over three or four lines ("4. Laws made under articles 2 and 3 to provide for the amendment of the First and the Fourth Schedules and ...") needed a small change to the section lister; it only triggers when the two-line forms do not match. The 1,474 earlier files were not touched (hashes unchanged, nothing re-embedded).
- Storage: the main Neon database stays about 694 MB (stop line 900 MB); 959 passages were embedded into the supplement vectors.

**Lookups** (all return the right unit first): "Article 21", "Art. 226 Constitution", "Article 32", "Art 14", "Article 21A", "Seventh Schedule", "Article 243ZH of the Constitution", "Article 370".

**Statute links in judgments** (the Constitution is now linked): judgments with at least one link **4,309 (13.1%) -> 5,525 (16.8%)**; 1,666 judgments now have a Constitution link; most-linked Articles: 14 (262 judgments), 32 (181), 226 (180), 19 (103), 136 (101), 16 (89). 20 random Constitution links checked by hand (seed 5): 20 of 20 correct.

**Five Articles to spot-check** against `constitution-of-india.pdf` (PDF page): Article 14 (page 37), Article 21 (page 42), Article 21A (page 42), Article 226 (pages 135 to 136), Article 243ZH (page 168). Also the Seventh Schedule (pages 341 to 355) and Eighth Schedule (page 356: 22 languages).

## 4. Shared limits
Chat, drafting, review and scan counters and the monthly all-browsers cap are rows in Supabase (`vault_usage`); the per-minute limits (searches 30, drafting retries 12, template picks 15) use `rate_hits` and the function `hit_rate`. Tested: 30 searches answered then 429; the count sits in Supabase and a separate caller of the same function is refused too (that is what a second server instance sees); another address is unaffected; a chat question is counted once and not counted when it fails; the page's "questions left" reads the Supabase count. Falls back to the old Neon counters / a per-process limiter only when Supabase is not configured or unreachable. Details: `docs/PAID-SWITCHOVER.md` 4f.

## 5. Chat precision
When the question names an exact provision, that unit goes first and sources from other Acts are dropped (up to 3 neighbours of the same Act stay). Re-test (real model): "What is Order 7 Rule 11?" 4 sources, all CPC, check *supported* (was 9 sources with Income-tax and Court Fees noise, check *check*); "What does Article 137 of the Limitation Act say?" 1 source, *supported*; "What does section 138 of the NI Act say?" *supported*; "What does Article 21 say?" says it cannot find the Article (the Constitution is not loaded, item 1).
