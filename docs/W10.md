## Judgment reader (long judgments)
The judgment text on SC case pages is now a reader (`JudgmentReader.tsx`; pure helpers `src/lib/judgments/readerSegments.mjs`, tested): the first 20 paragraphs, then **Show full judgment (N paragraphs)**, which adds the rest in steps of 25 paragraphs every ~25 ms so a phone never freezes (measured at 375 px on the 62,000-character judgment 2025_INSC_445, 235 paragraphs: full text in ~0.4 s, worst frame 17 ms, page height 6,870 px collapsed and 46,524 px expanded, no horizontal overflow). **Search within this judgment** (matches highlighted, the selected one stronger; Enter / arrows step through; typing "para 12" jumps to paragraph 12; a match in a paragraph not yet shown loads up to it first). Paragraph numbers ("12." or "(12)" at the start of a paragraph) get a small linked "¶ 12" label that is not part of the copied text. Links from search results (`#passage-N`) open the right passage even when it is far down. A floating "Back to top" button appears after scrolling. Once shown, the text is ordinary page text, so the browser's Find (Cmd/Ctrl+F), copy and print work on all of it; the controls are hidden in print. Until expanded, the page says so, and asks to choose Show full judgment first for Find or print. "Laws referred to" sits inside the same card.
