## Data
- **open-india-law**, Vaquill AI: Acts, regulator instruments and judgment text, compiled from India Code and regulator websites. Licence **CC BY 4.0**. https://huggingface.co/datasets/vaquill/open-india-law
- **Judgments**: source acknowledgment eCourts and the Supreme Court of India; source PDFs published on the AWS Open Data Registry by Dattam Labs (CC BY 4.0). High Court case metadata for some court-years comes from that registry's metadata files.
- **India Code** (Legislative Department, Government of India): `content/supplement/` contains section text copied by hand from India Code's Act PDFs. Written permission to republish this text has been received (details in `docs/PERMISSIONS.md`).
